# Changelog

## v1.0.0 (2026-10-21)
- CEICA on the web: it runs in your browser, with nothing to install.
- Nothing you enter is sent anywhere; your work is saved in this browser.
- Works offline after the first visit, and installs as an app in Edge or Chrome.
- Updates install themselves; Reload to update saves and reopens your project.
- Save still downloads the project file, the copy to keep and share.
- Reports, figures, tables and the Excel calculator are made in the browser.
- The same results and files as v0.1.0, checked file by file.

## v0.1.0 (2026-09-27)
- CE/ICA that follows IWR Planning Suite II, ties and duplicates included.
- Average annual outputs and costs, with interest during construction and OMRRR.
- Watershed plans from every combination of site actions, rules included.
- Decisions: recommendations, final array and TSP, flagged when results change.
- Benefit modules: habitat units, index x quantity, average values or STAF.
- STAF: function scores roll up to the ECI; import SFARI, EASI or DEEP exports.
- Inputs: paste from Excel, with undo, redo and autosave in the browser.
- Report: every figure and table, a Word or PDF document, or all in one .zip.
- Excel calculator with live formulas; edit it and import it back.
- Examples: Utoy Creek as published, and a STAF demonstration.
